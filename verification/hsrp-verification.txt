========================================
10. TROUBLESHOOTING AND DESIGN IMPROVEMENT
========================================

The original HSRP configuration used inconsistent group numbering.

Original examples included:

VLAN 10
IPv4 group 1
IPv6 group 2

VLAN 20
IPv4 group 2
IPv6 group 5

VLAN 30
IPv4 group 3
IPv6 group 2

VLAN 99
IPv4 group 4
IPv6 group 2


The IPv4 and IPv6 Active gateway placement also differed on some
VLANs.

During IPv6 testing, some inter-VLAN traffic failed.

The design was rebuilt using:

IPv4 group = VLAN ID

IPv6 group = VLAN ID + 100


The preferred Active gateway was also aligned with the spanning-tree
root.

After rebuilding VLAN 10 and VLAN 20, previously failing IPv6
inter-VLAN tests succeeded.

VLANs 30 and 99 were then migrated to the same convention.

Final verification across all four VLANs succeeded.


Troubleshooting conclusion:

The earlier IPv6 forwarding failure was not an inherent HSRPv2
forwarding limitation of the GNS3/IOSvL2 environment.

The standardized HSRP design restored successful IPv6 forwarding
and produced a simpler, more predictable dual-stack architecture.
